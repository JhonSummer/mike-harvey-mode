**Brief-fit score** is a heuristic, not a listen: + for trailer/cinematic/documentary/authoritative/deep/warm/premium/brand/commercial keywords, American/neutral/British accent, middle-aged, narrative/ad/TV use case, and clone count (log); − for whisper/ASMR/cute/kid/influencer/customer-support keywords, young, and characters/conversational use. Indian and other regional accents are pushed down (the brief asks for American or global-neutral, and the rejected Joshi take was Indian-accented). Nothing here has been auditioned yet.

Shared voices must be added to the account before TTS: `POST https://api.elevenlabs.io/v1/voices/add/{public_owner_id}/{voice_id}` with `{"new_name": "..."}`.
